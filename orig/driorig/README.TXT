These are the last of my DRI "original" disks with factory labels.

I used 22disk to transfer the files to DOS.  22Disk renames CP/M .COM
files to .CPM.

I also used Teledisk v2.15 to create disk images of all of the disks.

I also scanned the disks and included the scans.

Two of the disks are OEM master disks (Pascal MT+ & CB86).
